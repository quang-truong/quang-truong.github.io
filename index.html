Bio

I am a second-year Ph.D. Candidate at Thayer School of Engineering, Dartmouth College. I graduated summa cum laude in Computer Science at Texas Christian University.

I am broadly interested in graph-related problems. My area of specialization is at the intersections of Machine Learning on Graphs, Higher-order Graph Neural Networks, and Topological Deep Learning. In the past, I had opportunities to participate in Computer Vision projects, specifically Vehicle Re-identification and 3D Object Detection.
